Run configuration
-----------------
Reused verbatim for every post-optimization comparison so that results
stay directly comparable.

    python3 bm_raytrace/run_benchmark.py \
        -o results/raytrace_baseline.json \
        -p 10 -w 1 -n 5

    -p 10   10 independent worker processes
    -w 1    1 warmup value per process (discarded)
    -n 5    5 measured values per process

Image geometry is the benchmark default, 100x100 pixels
(DEFAULT_WIDTH / DEFAULT_HEIGHT in run_benchmark.py).

Result
------
raytrace: Mean +- std dev: 799 ms +- 4 ms

Interpretation
--------------
Relative standard deviation is approximately 0.5%, so run-to-run noise
is negligible compared with the >=7% improvement the project requires.
A saving of >=56 ms is therefore measurable with confidence, and any
apparent gain smaller than roughly 12 ms (3 sigma) should be treated as
noise rather than a real improvement.
